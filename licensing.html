What you always own

Your voice & lyrics

100% of your vocal performance, lyrics, and the master recording of your new song are yours at every tier — no exceptions.

No publishing grab on leases

MP3, WAV, and Trackout leases carry zero publishing split. We keep the beat composition; you keep everything you create on top of it.

Producer credit only

"Prod. AWA" on your release credits is the only ongoing condition. That's it — no recurring fees, no check-ins, no restrictions on where you release.

Prior lessees protected

If someone later buys an exclusive, any lease you already purchased stays valid. Your rights don't expire because of someone else's purchase.

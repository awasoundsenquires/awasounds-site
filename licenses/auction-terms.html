1. What the Vault Drop is

The Vault Drop is a timed online auction hosted on awasounds.com in which registered users compete to purchase exclusive beats, cover art, and cover art videos ("Items"). Each Item is a one-of-one exclusive — it will not be re-listed or sold to any other buyer once the auction closes. Items are sold as described; no physical goods are exchanged unless separately agreed.

2. AWA Credits — what they are and what they are not

  • AWA Credits ("Credits") are a virtual activity currency issued by Awa Sounds. They exist solely to enable participation in Vault Drop auctions and related platform features.
  • Credits are not money, not a store of value, and not redeemable for cash under any circumstances. They have no monetary face value.
  • Credits grant the right to place bids. They do not constitute payment for an Item. The winning buyer must pay the closing price in real GBP after the auction ends (see Section 6).
  • Each bid costs 5 Credits. This cost is deducted at the moment the bid is placed and is non-refundable regardless of outcome — including if you are outbid or if the auction closes without a winner.
  • Credits have no expiry while your account is active. Credits are personal to your account and may not be transferred, gifted, or sold to another user.

3. How Credits are obtained

  • Free allocation: every registered account receives 20 free Credits at the start of each calendar month. These do not roll over — unused free Credits expire at month end.
  • Purchased Credits: additional Credits may be purchased at any time via the Credits page or directly during a live auction. Purchased Credits do not expire while your account is active.
  • Awa Sounds reserves the right to adjust the monthly free allocation and credit pricing with 14 days' notice.
  • Credit purchases are final. No refunds on purchased Credits, except where required by UK consumer law.

4. Eligibility to bid

  • You must be a registered Awa Sounds account holder aged 16 or over.
  • You must have a sufficient Credit balance at the moment of each bid (minimum 5 Credits per bid).
  • You must be able and willing to pay the closing price in GBP if you win. By bidding you confirm this.
  • Accounts under suspension or payment default are ineligible to bid.

5. How bidding works

  • Each auction item has a starting bid (minimum 50 Credits) and a minimum increment of 5 Credits per bid.
  • Every bid placed costs 5 Credits from your balance, in addition to the bid amount itself. Example: placing a bid of 80 Credits costs you 80 Credits as your stated bid amount, and 5 Credits as the bid fee — total 85 Credits deducted. Only the bid amount counts toward the closing price calculation.
  • The closing price is the highest bid amount in Credits at auction end, converted to GBP at the published rate at the time the auction closes.
  • Anti-snipe extension: any bid placed in the final 5 minutes of an auction automatically extends the timer by 5 minutes. Extensions continue until 5 full minutes pass with no new bids.
  • All bid amounts and usernames are visible to all participants in real time.
  • Bids are binding. Once placed, a bid cannot be withdrawn.

6. Winning and payment

  • The auction closes when the timer reaches zero with no further anti-snipe extension. The highest bidder at that moment is the winner.
  • The winner is notified by email within 15 minutes of the auction closing.
  • The winner must complete payment of the closing price in GBP within 48 hours of receiving the winner notification. Payment is made via the checkout link provided in the notification email.
  • If payment is not received within 48 hours, the win is forfeited. The item may be re-listed or offered to the next highest bidder at Awa Sounds' discretion.
  • Credits spent bidding are not refunded in the event of a forfeited win.
  • Upon receipt of payment, the purchased licence (Cover Art, Cover Art Video, or Beat Lease as applicable) and the digital file(s) are issued to the winner within 24 hours.

7. GBP conversion rate

The Credit-to-GBP closing price is calculated at a published rate set by Awa Sounds before each auction begins and displayed prominently on the auction page. The rate is fixed for the duration of that auction and does not change once bidding opens. Example: if the rate is 10 Credits = £1, a winning bid of 200 Credits results in a closing price of £20 GBP.

8. Licence attached to the winning item

All Items sold via Vault Drop carry the applicable Awa Sounds licence:

  • Beats: the licence tier (MP3, WAV, Trackout, or Exclusive) is specified on each auction listing page.
  • Cover Art: governed by the Cover Art Licence — single-use exclusive, mandatory @awasounds Instagram credit.
  • Cover Art Video: governed by the Cover Art Video Licence — single-use exclusive, mandatory @awasounds Instagram credit on all public posts.

The winner's licence is issued with their name and the date of the auction as the licence date.

9. Cancellation and refunds

  • Winning payments are non-refundable once the digital file has been delivered, in accordance with the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 (digital content exemption). The buyer is notified of this before completing payment.
  • If Awa Sounds cancels an auction before it closes, all Credits spent bidding in that auction are refunded in full to all participants.
  • If a technical error causes a bid to be placed incorrectly, contact us within 24 hours at awasound.music@gmail.com. We will investigate and, where an error is confirmed, restore the Credits.

10. Prohibited conduct

  • Bid manipulation: using multiple accounts, coordinating with other users to suppress or inflate bids, or any form of collusion is strictly prohibited and will result in permanent account suspension and forfeiture of all Credits.
  • Shill bidding: Awa Sounds staff and associates do not bid in Vault Drop auctions.
  • Chargebacks: initiating a chargeback or payment dispute after receiving a digital file is considered fraud and will result in account suspension and legal recovery action.

11. Gambling Act 2005 compliance

The Vault Drop is structured as a timed auction, not a lottery, raffle, or prize competition. AWA Credits are a non-monetary virtual activity currency and do not constitute consideration in the legal sense of gambling legislation. The winning buyer pays the closing price in real GBP — the Credits determine bidding order and activity, not payment. Awa Sounds has designed this system to comply with the Gambling Act 2005 and the UK Consumer Rights Act 2015. If you have any concerns, contact us at awasound.music@gmail.com.

12. Disputes

Any dispute arising from a Vault Drop auction that cannot be resolved informally within 7 days will be subject to the governing law and jurisdiction set out in the Awa Sounds Terms & Conditions (England and Wales).

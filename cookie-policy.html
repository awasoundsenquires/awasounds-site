Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-23. This policy explains how Awa Sounds uses cookies and similar technologies on awasounds.com in line with UK PECR (Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations) and UK GDPR.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember information about your visit — like whether you are logged in.

2. Cookies we use

Cookie name / typePurposeDurationRequired?
sb-[ref]-auth-token Supabase authentication session — keeps you logged in to your Awa Sounds account. Session / up to 7 days Yes — strictly necessary
cookie_consent Remembers your cookie consent choice so we do not ask again. 12 months Yes — strictly necessary
Analytics (future) Anonymised page-view data to improve the site. Not active yet — we will ask for consent before enabling. Up to 26 months No — consent required

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. We do not share cookie data with social media platforms or ad networks.

3. Your choices

  • Accept / Decline: when you first visit the Site, a banner asks for your consent to any non-essential cookies. You can change your choice at any time via the "Cookie settings" link in the footer.
  • Browser settings: you can block or delete cookies at any time through your browser's settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent account login from working.
  • Opt out of analytics: if and when we enable analytics, you will have a separate opt-out option.

4. Third-party cookies

Our payment processor (GoDaddy Payments / Stripe) and authentication provider (Supabase) may set their own cookies during transactions or login. These are governed by their respective privacy policies, not ours.

5. Updates

We will update this policy when we introduce new cookies or change how existing ones are used. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the current version.
